Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, operating, and returning farm equipment with AgroRent.

Are tractor drivers or certified operators provided with the equipment?

Yes! You can choose between "Self-Operate" (if you or your family member has valid tractor operating experience) or opt for our verified, certified tractor operators. Our operators are trained in precision tillage, laser leveling, zero-till sowing, and fuel-efficient maneuvering.

How are rental operating hours measured and billed?

Every tractor in our fleet is fitted with a tamper-proof digital GPS engine telemetry hour meter. Billing is calculated strictly on actual working meter hours from the moment work begins on your field, ensuring 100% transparency with zero idle-time penalties.

What happens if an equipment encounters a breakdown in my field?

We provide a 24/7 Field Breakdown Guarantee. Our emergency mobile mechanic van is dispatched from the nearest rural hub within 2 hours. If mechanical snags cannot be resolved on-site within 60 minutes, a replacement tractor or implement is immediately dispatched so you never miss your weather window.

Can I rent specialized implements without renting a tractor?

Absolutely. If you already own a tractor, you can rent standalone implements such as rotavators, reversible MB ploughs, Happy Seeders, laser levellers, or boom sprayers. All implements feature standard Category I & II 3-point hitches compatible with Mahindra, Swaraj, John Deere, Sonalika, and Massey Ferguson tractors.

How is equipment delivered to remote agricultural fields?

We operate dedicated low-bed hydraulic carrier trailers that transport tractors and heavy implements directly to your field gate or village landmark within 2 hours of booking confirmation. You can track carrier arrival in real time.
