Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions regarding security deposits, diesel fuel, or breakdown protocols?

How is the refundable security deposit calculated and returned?
We charge a modest, nominal security deposit (between ₹880 for mini tractors and ₹1,500 for heavy tractors). Upon field completion and equipment handover, our pickup team performs a swift digital inspection and triggers an instant 100% deposit refund directly to your bank account or UPI within 4 hours.
What happens if the tractor or implement breaks down in my field?
AgroRent provides a strict 100% In-Field Breakdown Guarantee. Our mobile service van reaches your field within 2 hours. If mechanical trouble cannot be resolved within 3 hours, an equivalent replacement tractor is dispatched immediately at zero additional expense to you.
How does the diesel fuel policy work?
We operate on a "Full-to-Full" fuel policy. The machine arrives with a 100% full fuel tank. You may refill it before return or allow our technician to calculate fuel consumed according to the digital hour meter and pay actual local diesel rates.
Can I operate the tractor myself if I have an agricultural license?
Yes, absolutely! Self-operation is completely permitted. All you need to do is show your Aadhaar card and a valid tractor driving license at the time of machinery delivery. You do not need to pay any operator charges.
Can I extend my rental duration if rains delay my sowing?
Yes! You can extend your rental anytime with 1 click directly from your Farmer Dashboard or by calling our toll-free helpline 1800 456 7890.
