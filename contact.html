Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers to common inquiries about booking, delivery, and machinery support.

How can I book agricultural equipment?
You can easily book agricultural equipment online through our Booking page by selecting your machinery, rental dates, and village location. You can also submit the Rental Inquiry Form above, call our helpline directly at +91 98765 43210, or message us on WhatsApp. Our equipment coordination desk will confirm availability and finalize delivery.
Is doorstep delivery available?
Yes, we provide convenient doorstep and farm-gate delivery directly to your farmland across all our active regional service areas. Our specialized transport trailers ensure tractors, harvesters, and implements arrive on time and are safely unloaded at your designated field point.
Can I rent machinery without an operator?
Yes, self-drive rentals are available for experienced farmers and tractor operators holding a valid driving license. For farmers who prefer not to operate machinery themselves or need specialized work (such as laser levelling or combine harvesting), we provide certified professional operators upon request.
How are rental charges calculated?
Rental charges are calculated transparently in Indian Rupees (₹) based on flexible rental tiers: Hourly Rates (ideal for small field plots), Daily Rates (8 working hours per day), Weekly Plans (with a 15% discount), and Monthly Season Passes. There are zero hidden surge fees during peak harvest seasons.
Is a security deposit required?
Yes, a nominal refundable security deposit is collected prior to machinery dispatch to ensure safe usage. The deposit is 100% refunded immediately after post-rental machine inspection and return handover at your farm gate.
What happens if equipment breaks down?
In the rare event of mechanical failure or implement breakdown during field operations, our mobile field mechanics are dispatched promptly to perform on-site repairs. If on-field repair cannot be completed within 2 hours, we dispatch a replacement machine to ensure your farm work continues without interruption.
How can I check machinery availability?
You can check equipment availability in real time on our Equipment page, or reach out directly to our team via WhatsApp or phone at +91 98765 43210. During peak cultivation and harvest cycles, we recommend submitting an inquiry 2 to 3 days in advance to secure your preferred machine.
