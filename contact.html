Common Delivery & Support Queries

Contact & Service FAQs

Quick answers to frequently asked questions about delivery, visits, and customer assistance.

Can your transport trucks deliver tractors to remote farms with narrow dirt bunds?
Yes! Our hydraulic carrier transport trucks deliver equipment right up to the nearest motorable village pucca/kuccha road. From there, our certified driver carefully walks or drives the tractor directly across your farm approach bund onto your designated field coordinates.
What is the fastest way to share my farm location for delivery?
Simply send a WhatsApp message to +91 94123 45678 and click "Share Live Location" or "Location Pin" via Google Maps. Our GPS dispatch logistics team locks in your exact field coordinates and coordinates with the delivery driver.
Can I visit my nearest regional depot to inspect and test-drive the machinery?
You are always welcome! All our regional depots (Ludhiana, Karnal, Nashik, Indore, Coimbatore) are open 7 days a week from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM. You can inspect the engine, test the hydraulics, and speak with our senior mechanics before confirming your booking.
Are customer helpline and emergency mechanics active during night harvest shifts?
Yes! During peak Rabi and Kharif harvesting periods, our Combine Harvester and Heavy Tractor emergency support desks operate on a 24-hour round-the-clock basis. Mobile service vans remain on active field standby throughout the night.
