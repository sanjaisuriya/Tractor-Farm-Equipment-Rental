Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers to common inquiries about booking, delivery, and machinery support.

How can I book a tractor?
You can easily book a tractor by browsing our Equipment page and clicking "Book Equipment", or by filling out the Rental Inquiry Form on this page. You may also call our customer support team directly at +91 98765 43210 or message us on WhatsApp for fast booking assistance.
Can I rent equipment for multiple days?
Yes, we offer flexible rental durations ranging from single-day rentals to multi-day, weekly, and seasonal cultivation packages for tractors, seed drills, rotavators, and combine harvesters.
How do I check equipment availability?
You can check real-time equipment status on the Equipment catalog page, or contact our support team with your preferred dates, machinery type, and farm location to verify current fleet availability.
Can I cancel my equipment booking?
Yes, you can cancel or reschedule your equipment rental prior to dispatch by contacting customer support at +91 98765 43210 or via the Farmer Dashboard portal.
Is equipment delivery available to my village?
We provide delivery service to farm gates and accessible village points throughout our active regional service areas. Enter your village and district in the inquiry form so our team can verify the delivery route.
How can I contact customer support?
You can contact our customer support team by calling +91 98765 43210 (Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM), chatting via WhatsApp at +91 98765 43210, or sending an email to support@agrorent.in.
