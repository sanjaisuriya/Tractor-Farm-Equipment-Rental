Preparation Checklist

Equipment Rental Guidelines for Farmers

Quick preparation guidelines to ensure fast delivery, smooth implement hitching, and maximum field productivity on your rental day.

Required Verification

Keep a valid government ID (Aadhaar or Voter ID) and the registered mobile number handy for instant OTP verification upon machine arrival.

Field Path & Gate Width

Ensure an 8-foot minimum roadway or farm path width so our low-bed hydraulic carrier trailer can transport the tractor safely to your field gate.

Diesel Fuel Policy

All machinery is delivered with a 100% full fuel tank. Return the machine refueled or simply settle fuel costs at the standard local pump rate.

Implement Hitch Calibration

Our delivery technician calibrates 3-point linkages, checks PTO spline safety locks, and tests hydraulic lifting before handover.

Common Questions

Booking & Rental FAQ

Answers to frequent questions about payments, cancellations, driver availability, and equipment deliveries.

Can I pay via Cash on Delivery (COD) or UPI when the tractor arrives?

Yes! You can complete payment using any UPI application (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking, or cash on delivery directly to our transport carrier supervisor upon machine inspection at your farm gate.

What happens if unexpected rain prevents me from working in the field?

We provide Weather Delay Protection. If rain or unworkable soil moisture prevents tilling, call our helpline or update via your Farmer Dashboard. Your booking will be rescheduled to the next available dry window with zero cancellation fees.

Are operator / driver charges mandatory or optional?

Operator charges are 100% optional. If you or your family member can operate the machine safely, uncheck the "Need Certified Operator" box to pay only for the equipment. If you require an experienced driver, our certified operators handle all fieldwork.

How soon will the equipment arrive after booking?

For on-demand bookings within our 120+ rural hub coverage areas, equipment is dispatched within 2 hours. For scheduled future dates, our trailer arrives promptly at your selected start time.
