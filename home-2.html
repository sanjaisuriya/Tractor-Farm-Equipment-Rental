Frequently Asked Rental Questions

Quick, honest answers to common questions about rental packages, weather delays, operators, and machine maintenance.

What happens if rain or weather delays my farming?

We offer weather protection rollover! Simply notify our dispatch coordinator via WhatsApp or phone. Unused booked hours can be rescheduled to the next sunny window without extra fees.

Who pays for repairs if machinery breaks down in the field?

100% of mechanical maintenance and routine wear-and-tear is covered by AgroRent. Our mobile service vans resolve field issues within 60 minutes, or we dispatch a replacement machine.

How is the working time measured for hourly or daily rentals?

Every tractor features a tamper-proof digital engine hour-meter. Billing begins only when the machine commences active work on your farm acreage, excluding transport transit time.

What documents are required to book rental machinery?

Booking is fast and farmer-friendly: simply provide a valid Aadhaar Card or Kisan Credit Card (KCC) along with your village and field landmark. No complex paperwork or heavy deposits needed.

Have specific farm equipment questions?

Our agricultural specialists are available 7 AM to 8 PM daily.
